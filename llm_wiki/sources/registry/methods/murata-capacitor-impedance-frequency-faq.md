# Source Summary

## What It Covers

- Murata states that capacitor impedance magnitude and ESR vary with frequency
- Murata frames this as capacitor `frequency characteristics`
- Murata notes that high-frequency behavior differs by capacitor type, material, and shape

## Why It Matters

- Supports a conservative local boundary that real capacitors are frequency-dependent components rather than ideal capacitors
- Anchors `ESR` and impedance-frequency vocabulary before any cookbook value or placement claims are made

## Extraction Notes

- Safe for high-level statements that capacitor impedance and ESR vary with frequency
- Safe for vendor-scoped wording that MLCCs can provide low impedance at high frequencies
- Do not use this FAQ for universal dielectric recommendations, exact SRF values, impedance targets, or decoupling recipes
