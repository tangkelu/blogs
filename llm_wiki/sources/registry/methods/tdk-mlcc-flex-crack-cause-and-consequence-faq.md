# Source Summary

## What It Covers

- TDK identifies severe bending or flexure of the board during manufacturing or assembly as a common cause of MLCC flex cracks.
- TDK explains that the crack can start as an open-defect mode and, if the body resistance degrades, can progress toward a short-defect mode later.
- TDK warns that this latent progression can create serious downstream failure consequences.

## Why It Matters

- Gives the local corpus one official source for the `latent damage now, short/open later` part of the dense-PCBA probing failure chain.
- Supports public writing that explains why a board may initially pass electrical screening yet still carry hidden MLCC damage into the field.
