# Source Summary

## What It Covers

- Public document identity for `IPC-2223E`
- Public design-scope framing for flexible and rigid-flexible printed boards
- Public TOC headings that expose at least part of the board-type taxonomy, including `Type 1 Single-Sided Flexible Printed Boards`

## Why It Matters

- It gives the `E6` article lane one official standards-owner anchor for flex-board type naming without depending on article wording.
- It helps keep `single-sided`, `double-sided`, `multilayer flex`, and `rigid-flex` taxonomy separate from material, bend, and capability claims.

## Extraction Notes

- Safe for document name, revision identity, high-level flex / rigid-flex design scope, and the public type-taxonomy headings visible in the TOC.
- Pair with `ipc-6013e-toc` when the prompt needs the fuller public list of `Type 1` through `Type 5` flex-board families.
- Do not extract or invent bend-radius tables, conductor geometry, adhesive rules, coverlay rules, plated-hole rules, or qualification thresholds from this public TOC alone.
