## Safe Claim Families Learned

- assembly-oriented DFM/DFA review prevents unbuildable or unrepairable boards
- clearance, access, and depanelization are multi-factor assembly risks
- paste, stencil, and mark-point design affect print transfer and placement recognition
- BGA reliability depends on escape routing, via treatment, reflow, and inspection sequence
- DIP/THT fit depends on hole, pitch, and process compatibility
- silkscreen legibility and polarity clarity prevent misbuilds
- test strategy depends on fixtures, locating holes, and board readiness
